## Context

The product had outgrown its early architecture. Delivery slowed, reliability declined, and teams were hesitant to change core services.

## Approach

- Created a migration roadmap with risk-based sequencing
- Introduced automated testing gates and deployment checks
- Isolated high-churn modules behind stable interfaces

## Outcome

We improved reliability and throughput while keeping the business moving, enabling new features without regressing stability.
