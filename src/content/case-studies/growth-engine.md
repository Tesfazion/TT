## Context

Leads were scattered across tools, response times varied, and there was no single source of truth for pipeline health.

## Approach

- Mapped the customer journey and identified the highest-leverage automation points
- Built integrations to unify inbound sources and enrich lead data
- Implemented scoring and follow-up orchestration with clear dashboards

## Outcome

Teams regained visibility into pipeline health and focused on higher-quality opportunities with faster response times.
