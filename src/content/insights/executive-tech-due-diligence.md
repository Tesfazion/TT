## The goal

You don’t need to read every line of code to understand risk. You need signal.

## My 60-minute checklist

- **Business alignment:** what’s the product promise and what are the constraints?
- **Architecture:** where are the hard edges and failure modes?
- **Delivery system:** CI/CD, testing strategy, release cadence, incident practices
- **Team health:** ownership, decision-making, clarity of roles

## Outcome

At the end, you should be able to answer: *can this team reliably ship what the business needs in the next 90 days?*
