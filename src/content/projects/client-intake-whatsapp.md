## Why I built it

When someone messages “Hi” and nothing else, you spend time asking the same questions again and again.
I wanted an intake flow that feels friendly but still collects the important details up front.

## What it does

- Asks a few clear questions (service type, timeline, budget range, contact info)
- Routes the message to the right channel/person on WhatsApp
- Keeps a simple record so you can follow up later

## Small details I care about

- Short questions (people actually finish the form)
- Clear “next step” message after submission
- No spammy automation — just enough structure to move faster
