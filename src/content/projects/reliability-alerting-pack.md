## Why I looked into this

I’m interested in what happens after you deploy: how do you notice problems early, and how do you avoid noisy alerts?
This is a learning project where I collect simple patterns.

## What I focused on

- A small set of health checks that actually matter
- Alerts that are useful (not “always red”)
- Short runbooks: what to check first, where to look, how to recover
