## What this is

This is a starter design I use to learn how multi‑tenant apps are usually organized.
It’s not a “finished product” — more like a clean foundation: orgs, users, roles, and permissions.

## What I explored

- Tenant / organization structure
- RBAC basics (roles and permissions)
- Audit log ideas (who changed what, and when)

## Why it matters

Even small apps become messy when you add multiple customers. I wanted to understand the patterns early.
