## The idea

I wanted a “one screen” view for day‑to‑day operations: what’s in stock, what’s low, what’s pending, and what needs attention.
This is a dashboard concept I use to practice data + UI basics.

## What I focused on

- A layout that reads well on a laptop and on a phone
- Clear status labels (low stock, delayed, pending)
- Simple inputs (a spreadsheet or a small database)

## What I learned

- The hardest part is not charts — it’s clean data
- Simple tables + filters often beat complex dashboards
